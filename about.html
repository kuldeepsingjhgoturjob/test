About us

A dynamic recruitment and staffing company delivering Pan India hiring solutions.

Got UR Job is a dynamic recruitment and staffing company delivering Pan India hiring solutions. We connect businesses with qualified professionals through transparent, efficient and result-driven hiring practices, and we build long-term relationships that create real value for employers and meaningful career opportunities for professionals.

Our vision

To become one of India’s most trusted recruitment and staffing partners, delivering quality talent, transparent hiring solutions and exceptional client service.

Our mission

To deliver quality recruitment solutions across India, build long-term hiring partnerships, help businesses hire faster, create meaningful career opportunities, and maintain transparency in every recruitment process.

Our core values

01 Integrity

We conduct every business interaction with honesty, ethics and accountability.

02 Transparency

Clear, open communication is maintained throughout every step of the hiring process.

03 Quality

We carefully screen every candidate to ensure only the best talent reaches our clients.

04 Commitment

Our team provides dedicated, consistent support to every client and candidate.

05 Professionalism

We uphold the highest standards of excellence in all our recruitment services.

06 Partnership

We build relationships that go beyond placements, creating lasting value for all.

Founder message

My vision has always been simple: to make recruitment easier, faster and more human. We believe the right hire is not just about skills on a resume. It is about finding the right culture fit, the right mindset and the right potential.

At Got UR Job we are committed to transparency, professionalism and building long-term partnerships, with both our clients and the professionals we place. Every assignment we take on is handled with dedication and accountability.

Thank you for trusting us to be your hiring partner. Together, let’s build teams that grow businesses and shape careers.

Kuldeep Singh
Founder and CEO, Got UR Job
